# Test 22（第二十二章 倒装句）题目与答案

> 提取自 https://llwslc.github.io/grammar-club/content/Chapter22.html#test
> 题目、答案与解析取自该页面渲染组件所带数据；下划线/画底线以 `<u>…</u>` 标注，选择题空格以 `__` 标注。

## 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**1.** The students were warned that on no account __ to cheat.
- (A) they were
- (B) were they ✅
- (C) they should
- (D) they can
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** on no account 是否定副词短语，移至 that 从句句首即需倒装。

**2.** __ make up for lost time.
- (A) Only by working hard we can
- (B) By only working hard we can
- (C) Only by working hard can we ✅
- (D) By only working hard can we
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** only by working hard 因有 only 修饰，在句首要倒装。

**3.** Rarely __ such nonsense.
- (A) I have heard
- (B) have I heard ✅
- (C) I do hear
- (D) don't I hear
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** rarely 有否定功能，置于句首要倒装。

**4.** __ perched a large black bird.
- (A) Often
- (B) Suddenly
- (C) On the wire ✅
- (D) It
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 地方副词置于句首，类似 there is/are 的句型，方可倒装，故选 C。

**5.** Only just now __ to him about the things to heed while riding a motorcycle.
- (A) I talked
- (B) was I talking ✅
- (C) talked I
- (D) I was talked
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 因有 only just now 在句首，要倒装。

**6.** John was as confused about the rules __.
- (A) as were the other contestants ✅
- (B) as the other contestants had
- (C) than were the other contestants
- (D) than the other contestants had
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 前有 as confused，后面要有 as（A 或 B）。因为前面是 John was confused，有 be 动词，后面不能用 had 来代表，应用 be 动词，故选 A，这是比较级的倒装。

**7.** An IBM PC 286 is as powerful __ on NASA's Voyager II.
- (A) than the mainframe computer is
- (B) than is the mainframe computer
- (C) as the mainframe computer is powerful
- (D) as is the mainframe computer ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 上文 as 要求用 as 作连接，C 错在 powerful 不应重复。

**8.** The New Testament is a book __ the life and teachings of Jesus.
- (A) which can be found
- (B) in which can be found ✅
- (C) which can find
- (D) in which can find
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 原句是 The life and teachings of Jesus can be found in the book，改成关系从句再倒装，即是 B。

**9.** Not until the doctor was sure everything was all right __ the emergency room.
- (A) he left
- (B) left he
- (C) did he leave ✅
- (D) he did leave
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** not until 移到句首要用倒装句型。

**10.** __, man could die out.
- (A) World War III should ever break out
- (B) If should World War III ever break out
- (C) If World War III should have broken out
- (D) Should World War III ever break out ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 原句是 If World War Ⅲ should ever break out… 省略 If 后倒装即是 D。

**11.** The results, __, the leading journal of science, indicate that the experimental procedure is flawed.
- (A) says Nature ✅
- (B) Nature says
- (C) which says Nature
- (D) which Nature says
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 原句是间接引句，Nature says the results… 改成倒装句成为 A 会比不倒装的 B 好，因为空格后的 the leading journal of science 是 Nature 的同位语，两者应该在一起。

**12.** Across the street from the station __.
- (A) stood an old drugstore ✅
- (B) it stood an old drugstore
- (C) where an old drugstore stood
- (D) which stood an old drugstore
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 地方副词 across the street from the station 移到句首而成倒装句，类似 there is/are 的句型。

**13.** I tried to call some friends but __.
- (A) none could I reach ✅
- (B) could I reach none
- (C) I could none reach
- (D) I none could reach
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 是 I could reach none 的倒装。

**14.** __ trouble you again.
- (A) Never will I ✅
- (B) Not I will ever
- (C) Will not ever I
- (D) Never I will
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 是 I will never trouble you again 的倒装句。

**15.** Not until you paint your first oil color __ the difference between theory and practice.
- (A) you find out
- (B) and find out
- (C) finding out
- (D) do you find out ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** not until 移到句首要倒装。

**16.** __ a baby deer is born, it struggles to stand on its own feet.
- (A) No sooner
- (B) As soon as ✅
- (C) So soon as
- (D) Not sooner that
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 答案 A 要用倒装句，C 和 D 都不是正确的连接词，只有 B 能引导后面那个没有倒装的从句。

**17.** __ the invention of the movable print, books were mostly copied by hand and cost far more than ordinary people could afford.
- (A) After
- (B) Until ✅
- (C) Not until
- (D) Because of
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** “活版印刷发明前，书原来都是用手抄，一般人根本买不起。”从句意来看，只有 until 符合。

**18.** __ did I find out that he was dead.
- (A) A moment ago
- (B) Only a moment ago ✅
- (C) An only moment ago
- (D) For a moment
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 下文是倒装句，所以选择要求倒装的 only。

**19.** Henry James is __ is his philosopher brother William.
- (A) famous and also
- (B) as famous as ✅
- (C) famous so
- (D) equally famous
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 比较级后面倒装了。

**20.** __ does the recluse venture out of his hermitage.
- (A) Seldom ✅
- (B) Often
- (C) Occasionally
- (D) Sometimes
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 下文是倒装句，所以选择要求倒装的 seldom。
