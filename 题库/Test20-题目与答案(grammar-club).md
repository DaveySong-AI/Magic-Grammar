## 练习一 — 将下列各句中的副词从句（即画底线部分）改写为简化从句：

**1.** 题目：1. <u>After he was told to report to his supervisor</u>, the clerk left in a hurry.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>(Having been) told to report to his supervisor</u>, the clerk left in a hurry.

**2.** 题目：2. <u>Although he was ordered to leave</u>, the soldier did not move an inch.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>Although ordered to leave</u>, the soldier did not move an inch.

**3.** 题目：3. The plan must be modified <u>before it is put into effect</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：The plan must be modified <u>before being put into effect</u>.

**4.** 题目：4. <u>Because it had been bombed twice in the previous week</u>, the village was a total wreck.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>(Having been) bombed twice in the previous week</u>, the village was a total wreck.

**5.** 题目：5. <u>When all things are considered</u>, I cannot truly say that this was an accident.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>All things considered</u>, I cannot truly say that this was an accident.

**6.** 题目：6. <u>When the job was done</u>, the secretary went home.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>The job done</u>, the secretary went home.

**7.** 题目：7. He took on two extra jobs <u>so that he could feed his family</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：He took on two extra jobs <u>(so as) to feed his family</u>.

**8.** 题目：8. <u>If you are in doubt</u>, you should look up the word in the dictionary.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>If in doubt</u>, you should look up the word in the dictionary.

**9.** 题目：9. <u>Because pork is so expensive</u>, I'm buying beef instead.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>With pork so expensive</u>, I'm buying beef instead. 
 或 
 <u>Pork being so expensive</u>, I'm buying beef instead.

**10.** 题目：10. <u>When we consider his handicap</u>, he has done very well indeed.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>Considering his handicap</u>, he has done very well indeed.

## 练习二 — 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**11.** __ not a big star, the actor played in hundreds of films.
- (A) Although ✅
- (B) He was
- (C) Because
- (D) Despite
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 副词从句 Although he was not a big star 的简化。

**12.** Eisenhauer was president of Columbia University __ President of the USA.
- (A) before he becomes
- (B) before becoming ✅
- (C) before
- (D) before became
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 副词从句 before he became President of the USA 的简化。

**13.** Gold remains stable even __ to extremely high temperatures.
- (A) when is heated
- (B) it is heated
- (C) when to heat
- (D) when heated ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 副词从句 even when it is heated… 的简化。

**14.** __, the stock market crashed.
- (A) With investor confidence gone ✅
- (B) When investor confidence gone
- (C) When investors lose confidence
- (D) With investors lost confidence
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 副词从句 Because investor confidence was gone 简化成介系词短语。

**15.** A monkey's brain is small __ with the human brain.
- (A) when they are compared
- (B) when compare
- (C) compared ✅
- (D) to compare them
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 副词从句 when it is compared… 的简化。

**16.** Picasso did many of his abstract paintings __ living in Paris.
- (A) that he was
- (B) during
- (C) while ✅
- (D) and
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 副词从句 while he was living… 的简化。

**17.** __ at correct angles, diamonds reflect light brilliantly.
- (A) When carved ✅
- (B) If it is carved
- (C) Carving
- (D) If carving
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 副词从句 When they are carved… 的简化。

**18.** __, the children gradually learned to be independent.
- (A) Because their father gone
- (B) Their father was gone
- (C) Due to their father was gone
- (D) With their father gone ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 副词从句 Because their father was gone 简化为介系词短语。

**19.** She broke into tears __ the news.
- (A) upon hearing ✅
- (B) because hearing
- (C) when heard
- (D) when she hears
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 副词从句 as soon as she heard the news 简化为介系词短语。

**20.** __ the truth, I know nothing about it.
- (A) To tell you ✅
- (B) Telling you
- (C) I tell you
- (D) I told you
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 副词从句 If I can tell you the truth 的简化。
