# Test 11（第十一章 主语动词一致性）题目与答案

> 来源：参考 `语法俱乐部题库-修正版.md` 第十一章（Ex.1–Ex.55），该章原题型为「选出正确的主谓一致性形式」。本文件按你的要求**改版为二选一选择题**：每题 2 个选项 = 正确答案 + 其单数/复数对照的干扰项（如 is/are、has/have、make/makes、want/wants），正确答案保留。

> 原书题目嵌入各小节正文，共 55 题，括弧内为备选动词原形；本文件以 `____` 表示待填动词位置。


## 一、主语是一个还是两个人（或物）？

**Ex.1**　Your brother John ____ come to see you.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：A** has
**解析：** 「brother John」是同位语，指同一个人，单数主语，动词用第三人称单数 has。

**Ex.2**　Your brother and John ____ come to see you.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：B** have
**解析：** brother 与 John 是两个人，复数主语，动词用 have。

**Ex.3**　The senator and delegate ____ to make an announcement.

- A. wants
- B. want
**答案：A** wants
**解析：** 「senator and delegate」无冠词，指同一个人兼任两职，视为单数，用 wants。

**Ex.4**　The senator and the delegate ____ to make an announcement.

- A. wants
- B. want
**答案：B** want
**解析：** 两个定冠词，指两个人，复数主语，用 want。

**Ex.5**　Every man and every woman ____ to do something for the country.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：A** has
**解析：** every…and every… 强调整体中的每一人，仍视为单数，用 has。

**Ex.6**　All work and no play ____ Jack a dull boy.

- A. make
- B. makes
**答案：B** makes
**解析：** 「all work and no play」作为一句谚语的整体主语，单数概念，用 makes。

**Ex.7**　A cup and saucer ____ placed on the table.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** 「a cup and saucer」指一套（杯碟），单数，用 is。

**Ex.8**　A cup and a dish ____ placed on the table.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：B** are
**解析：** a cup 与 a dish 两样东西，复数，用 are。

**Ex.9**　A brown and white dog ____ at your doorsteps.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** 「a brown and white dog」一只狗（两种颜色），单数，用 is。

**Ex.10**　A brown and a white dog ____ fighting over a bone.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：B** are
**解析：** a brown (dog) 与 a white dog 两只狗，复数，用 are。

**Ex.11**　Bread and butter ____ not very tasty but very filling.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** 零冠词，指一种食品（涂奶油的吐司），单数，用 is。

**Ex.12**　Bread and butter ____ both risen in price.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：B** have
**解析：** 「双双涨价」指两种民生物资，视为复数，用 have。

**Ex.13**　Oil and water ____ not mix.

- A. do
- B. does
**答案：A** do
**解析：** 油和水是「两种」物质，复数主语，用 do。


## 二、主语是哪一个？

**Ex.14**　You want to borrow money? But I, as well as you, ____ broke.

- A. are
- B. am
**答案：B** am
**解析：** as well as you 是附带语，真正主语是 I，动词依 I 用 am。

**Ex.15**　I, no less than you, ____ responsible.

- A. am
- B. are
**答案：A** am
**解析：** no less than you 是附带语，真正主语是 I，用 am。

**Ex.16**　Everyone but a few complete idiots ____ able to see that.

- A. were
- B. was
**答案：B** was
**解析：** but 后否定部分排除，真正主语 everyone 为单数，用 was。

**Ex.17**　The eggs, not the hen, ____ stolen.

- A. were
- B. was
**答案：A** were
**解析：** not the hen 排除，主语 the eggs 为复数，用 were。

**Ex.18**　Not only you but also I ____ at fault.

- A. were
- B. was
**答案：B** was
**解析：** not only…but also 就近原则，靠近 I，用 was。

**Ex.19**　Either my father alone or both my parents ____ coming.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：A** are
**解析：** either…or 就近原则，靠近 both my parents（复数），用 are。

**Ex.20**　Neither he nor his friends ____ there at that time.

- A. was
- B. were
**答案：B** were
**解析：** neither…nor 就近原则，靠近 his friends（复数），用 were。

**Ex.21**　____ he or his friends want to go?

- A. Does
- B. Do
**答案：A** Does
**解析：** 疑问句助动词与 he（单数）一致，用 Does。（原书误作 Dose，应为 Does）

**Ex.22**　Everybody ____ to report here tomorrow.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** everybody 为单数不定代词，用 is。

**Ex.23**　Every student ____ several chapters to report on.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：A** has
**解析：** every + 单数名词，单数主语，用 has。

**Ex.24**　Each ____ to make a five-minute speech.

- A. have
- B. has
**答案：B** has
**解析：** each 为单数，用 has。

**Ex.25**　You ____ to make a five-minute speech each.

- A. have
- B. has
**答案：A** have
**解析：** 主语 you（对称/复数），用 have。

**Ex.26**　Each of you ____ responsible for half of the job.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** each of you 真正主语是 each（单数），用 is。


## 三、主语是关系代名词时

**Ex.27**　I don't trust people who ____ too much.

- A. talk
- B. talks
**答案：A** talk
**解析：** who 指代 people（复数），用 talk。

**Ex.28**　He has three options, which ____ equally attractive.

- A. looks
- B. look
**答案：B** look
**解析：** which 指代 three options（复数），用 look。

**Ex.29**　He has three options, which ____ a good thing.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** which 指代前面整件事（单数概念），用 is。

**Ex.30**　It ____ the Johnson boys who ____ here last night.

- A. were ... was
- B. was ... were
**答案：B** was ... were
**解析：** 强调句：It 为单数主语，be 用 was；who 从句主语 the Johnson boys（复数），be 用 were。


## 四、以单位做主语时

**Ex.31**　He makes <u>eighty thousand dollars</u> a year, which ____ a lot of money.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** which 指代 eighty thousand dollars 这笔钱（单数概念），用 is。

**Ex.32**　<u>Ten seconds</u> ____ quite a record for the 100-meter dash.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** 时间单位 ten seconds 视为一个整体时段，单数，用 is。


## 五、主语后面有介词短语时

**Ex.33**　Mrs. Lindsey, together with her sons, ____ on a European tour.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** together with her sons 是附带语，主语 Mrs. Lindsey（单数），用 is。

**Ex.34**　The use of computers in business ____ now almost inevitable.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** 真正主语是 use（单数），of computers 只是介词短语，用 is。

**Ex.35**　There ____ a list of things to buy in the handbag.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** there be 就近原则，主语 a list（单数），用 is。

**Ex.36**　All of these ____ Lishan pears.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：B** are
**解析：** these 为复数，用 are。

**Ex.37**　All of the money ____ been spent.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：A** has
**解析：** money 不可数、单数，用 has。

**Ex.38**　All but one of the pears ____ ripe.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：B** are
**解析：** but one 排除一个，主语 pears（复数），用 are。

**Ex.39**　A lot of the pears ____ damaged.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：A** are
**解析：** pears 复数，用 are。

**Ex.40**　A lot of time ____ been wasted.

- A. have
- B. has
**答案：B** has
**解析：** time 不可数、单数，用 has。

**Ex.41**　Half of the pears still ____ good.

- A. look
- B. looks
**答案：A** look
**解析：** pears 复数，用 look。

**Ex.42**　Half of this pear ____ rotten.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** this pear 单数，用 is。

**Ex.43**　Some of the cost ____ in transportation.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** cost 单数，用 is。

**Ex.44**　None of the pears ____ really good to eat.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：A 或 B 均可** is（两种形式皆可接受）
**解析：** none 作否定代词，单复数视语境；pears 可数，is / are 皆可。

**Ex.45**　The number of people in the demonstration ____ five thousand.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** the number of + 复数名词，主语是 number（单数），用 is。

**Ex.46**　A number of people ____ brought eggs to throw.

- A. has
- B. have
**答案：B** have
**解析：** a number of + 复数名词，主语是 people（复数），用 have。

**Ex.47**　A pair of pants ____ hanging on the wall.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A** is
**解析：** a pair 为单数单位，用 is。

**Ex.48**　These pants ____ very fancy.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：B** are
**解析：** pants 本身是复数名词，用 are。


## 六、集合名词

**Ex.49**　The committee ____ studying the proposal.

- A. is
- B. are
**答案：A 或 B 均可** is（两种形式皆可接受）
**解析：** 集合名词 committee 强调整体用 is，强调成员用 are，两者皆可。

**Ex.50**　The committee ____ five years old.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** 此处指委员会这个机构（整体）的「年龄」，用 is。

**Ex.51**　The committee ____ mostly Republican politicians.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：A** are
**解析：** 此处强调成员（多为共和党政客），用 are。


## 七、一些以 S 结尾的名词

**Ex.52**　Mathematics ____ my forte.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：B** is
**解析：** 学科名 mathematics 作单数，用 is。

**Ex.53**　Mumps primarily ____ children.

- A. attacks
- B. attack
**答案：A** attacks
**解析：** 疾病名 mumps 作单数，用 attacks。

**Ex.54**　Statistics ____ born in the gambling house.

- A. were
- B. was
**答案：B** was
**解析：** statistics 此处指「统计学」这门学科（单数），用 was。

**Ex.55**　The statistics ____ not all accurate.

- A. are
- B. is
**答案：A** are
**解析：** statistics 此处指「统计数据」（复数），用 are。
