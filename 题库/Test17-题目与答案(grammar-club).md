# Test 17（第十七章 形容词从句简化）题目与答案

## 练习一 — 将下列各句中的关系从句（即画底线部分）改写为简化从句：

**1.** 题目：1. Medieval suits of armor, <u>which were developed for protection during battle</u>, are now placed in castles for decoration.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：Medieval suits of armor, <u>developed for protection during battle</u>, are now placed in castles for decoration.

**2.** 题目：2. The change of style in these paintings should be obvious to anyone <u>that is familiar with the artist's works</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：The change of style in these paintings should be obvious to anyone <u>familiar with the artist's works</u>.

**3.** 题目：3. Islands are actually tips of underwater mountain peaks <u>that rise above water</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：Islands are actually tips of underwater mountain peaks <u>rising above water</u>.

**4.** 题目：4. John Milton, <u>who was author of Paradise Lost</u>, was a key member of Oliver Cromwell's cabinet.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：John Milton, <u>author of Paradise Lost</u>, was a key member of Oliver Cromwell's cabinet.

**5.** 题目：5. The secretary thought that it might not be the best time <u>that she should ask her boss for a raise</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：The secretary thought that it might not be the best time <u>to ask her boss for a raise</u>.

**6.** 题目：6. Gold is one of the heaviest metals <u>that are known to man</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：Gold is one of the heaviest metals <u>known to man</u>.

**7.** 题目：7. Here are some books <u>that your brother can use</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：Here are some books <u>for your brother to use</u>.

**8.** 题目：8. Sexual harassment, <u>which is a hotly debated issue in the work place</u>, will be the topic of the intercollegiate debate next week.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：Sexual harassment, <u>a hotly debated issue in the work place</u>, will be the topic of the intercollegiate debate next week.

**9.** 题目：9. There's nothing left <u>that I can say now</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：There's nothing left <u>(for me) to say now</u>.

**10.** 题目：10. People <u>that live along the waterfront</u> must be evacuated before the storm hits.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：People <u>living along the waterfront</u> must be evacuated before the storm hits.

## 练习二 — 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**11.** __ often found in fruit and vegetables.
- (A) Vitamin C, a trace element that is
- (B) For vitamin C, a trace element to be
- (C) Vitamin C, a trace element, is ✅
- (D) Vitamin C, is that trace element
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 答案 C 的句型是 Vitamin C is often found in fruit and vegetables，中间再加上同位语 a trace element（微量元素），也就是关系从句 which is a trace element 的简化。

**12.** The most important fossil __ in East Africa was that of an ancient female, dubbed Lucy.
- (A) excavated ✅
- (B) was excavated
- (C) to excavate
- (D) excavating
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 that was excavated in East Africa,简化后即得 A。

**13.** Steve Jobs' vision of the personal computer greatly expanded the number of people __ the computer for business and for pleasure.
- (A) actively used
- (B) were using actively
- (C) actively using ✅
- (D) who actively using
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 who were actively using the computer…，简化成为 C。

**14.** The Amazon rain forests, __ the earth's lungs, convert carbon dioxide in the atmosphere back into oxygen.
- (A) functioning as ✅
- (B) which functioning as
- (C) functions as
- (D) functioned as
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 which functions as the earth's lungs，简化为 A。

**15.** Through a process __ coalescence, water droplets in clouds grow to a size large enough to fall to earth.
- (A) calls
- (B) to be called
- (C) calling
- (D) called ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 that is called coalescence，简化为 D.

**16.** If you are looking for investment advice, I know just the place __.
- (A) going
- (B) to go ✅
- (C) you to go
- (D) for you going
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 where you can go，简化为 B。

**17.** Penicillin, __ in the early 20th century, brought in the golden age of chemotherapy.
- (A) to be discovered
- (B) discovering
- (C) discovery was
- (D) discovered ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 which was discovered in the early 20th century，简化为 D。

**18.** Those are not words __.
- (A) to be taken seriously ✅
- (B) to take them seriously
- (C) taking seriously
- (D) are taken seriously
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 that should be taken seriously，简化为 A。

**19.** The mouse, like the keyboard, is a control device __ to a computer.
- (A) connected ✅
- (B) to connect it
- (C) and connect
- (D) that connect
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 that is connected to a computer，简化为 A。

**20.** An amendment to the Constitution __ in Harry Truman's tenure limits the US presidency to two terms.
- (A) passing
- (B) to pass
- (C) passed ✅
- (D) was passed
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 空格以下原为关系从句 that was passed in Harry Truman's tenure，简化为 C。
