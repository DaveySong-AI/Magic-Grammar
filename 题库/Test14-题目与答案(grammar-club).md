## 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**1.** Not long ago I wrote a letter to a friend, __ almost got us into a quarrel.
- (A) whom
- (B) where
- (C) which ✅
- (D) what
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 这个位置要能作 got 的主语，又要作连接词，因而是关系代名词（A、C 或 D）。 其中 A 是宾格不行，D 又需省去先行词，所以只剩 C 的 which，表示“写信这件事”或“这封信”险些引起争吵。

**2.** England, __ is justly proud of her poets, is today ranked behind the continent in poetic achievement.
- (A) which ✅
- (B) that
- (C) where
- (D) whom
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 空格中是 is 的主语（A 或 B），而在括弧性的逗号中不应用指示性的 that，故选 A。

**3.** You are the only friend __ he will listen to at all.
- (A) where
- (B) whom
- (C) which
- (D) that ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 先行词是 the only friend，有明显的指示功能，故关系词应用 that。

**4.** Choose the correct sentence:
- (A) I have bought a book, the cover of which bears a picture of The Hague. ✅
- (B) I have bought a book; the cover of which bears a picture of The Hague.
- (C) I have bought a book, the cover of which, bears a picture of The Hague.
- (D) I have bought a book, of which bears a picture of The Hague.
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** B 错在以分号区分关系从句和先行词，C 错在以逗号分隔主词 the cover of which 和动词 bears，D 错在用介系词短语 of which 作主语。

**5.** This is the one encyclopedia upon __ I can depend.
- (A) that
- (B) which ✅
- (C) what
- (D) it
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 虽然先行词 the one encyclopedia 也有明显的指示功能，但关系词出现在介系词后面（在此为 upon）时就不能借用 that，所以选 B。

**6.** __ likes good food and cheerful service would like the Regent Hotel.
- (A) Who that
- (B) Someone
- (C) Whoever ✅
- (D) Who
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 空格前面没有先行词，因而要选 whoever 这个省略先行词的关系词。

**7.** This custom, __, is slowly disappearing.
- (A) of many centuries ago origin
- (B) which originated many centuries ago ✅
- (C) with many centuries origin
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** A 和 C 都在名词 origin 前面加上了短语（many centuries ago 和 many centuries）来修饰，可是名词前面只能用单词的形容词来修饰，所以错误。B 是正确的形容词从句。

**11.** __ is elected President, corruption won't cease.
- (A) Whatever
- (B) Who
- (C) How
- (D) Whoever ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 空格前无先行词，只能选 A 或 D。而“当选总统”者应为“人”，故选 D。

**12.** Neither success nor money, to me at least, is the criterion __ we are to be judged.
- (A) which
- (B) to which
- (C) under which ✅
- (D) since which
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 关系从句可还原为 We are to be judged under the criterion.（我们应以此标准来被衡量。），因而改成关系从句要用 under which。

**13.** I'm afraid I'd never be able to see Jane again, __ very much.
- (A) that I love
- (B) I love
- (C) I love her
- (D) whom I love ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 空格以下原来是这一句：I love Jane very much，改成关系从句即为 whom I love very much。因为关系从句前面有逗号，所以 whom 不能省略。

**14.** Didn't you know that all __ is not gold?
- (A) which glitters
- (B) glitters
- (C) who glitters
- (D) that glitters ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** All that glitters is not gold.（会发亮的并不都是金子。）这是一句格言。关系从句 that glitters 之中的关系词应用 that，因为先行词 all 表示“全部”，是一个指示明确的范围，所以要用 that 来取代 which。

**15.** I have a present for __ his hand first.
- (A) whoever raises ✅
- (B) whomever raises
- (C) anyone raises
- (D) whoever that raises
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** whoever 这种关系词不需要先行词，功能相当于 anyone that。因为要作动词 raises 的主语，所以要用主格 whoever。

**16.** Boys __ in the dorm make a lot of friends.
- (A) who live ✅
- (B) who lives
- (C) live
- (D) that living
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** who live in the dorm 是形容词从句，主语 who 代表先行词 boys，是复数， 所以动词 live 不加 -s。

**17.** The final decision will be up to __ everyone trusts.
- (A) Judge Clemens, whom ✅
- (B) Judge Clemens, who
- (C) Judge Clemens whom
- (D) Judge Clemens who
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** Judge Clemens 是专有名词，作为先行词时要用逗号隔开来，因为这种关系从句不能有指示性。逗号后面的关系词应用宾格的 whom，因为作 trusts 的宾语使用。

**18.** __ he has in his pocket, it's not a gun.
- (A) What
- (B) Whatever ✅
- (C) When
- (D) How
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** whatever 一字作为 no matter what 解释时，是表示让步的语气，后面引导的从句应作为表示让步的副词从句解释。

**19.** Abandoned flower pots are __.
- (A) where do mosquitoes thrive
- (B) mosquitoes thrive there
- (C) where mosquitoes thrive ✅
- (D) what mosquitoes thrive
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 本句可还原为：Abandoned flower pots are places where mosquitoes thrive.（弃置的花盆是蚊虫孳生的地方。）省略掉 places 之后就是 C 的答案。

**20.** The author wrote his first novel __ he was working as a hotel clerk.
- (A) which
- (B) during
- (C) what
- (D) while ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 空格后面是表示时间的副词从句，while 即是副词从句连接词。

**21.** __ held upside down, the fire extinguisher begins to spray bubbles.
- (A) When it is ✅
- (B) When they are
- (C) Whenever they are
- (D) During it is
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 后面的 fire extinguisher（灭火器）是单数，所以代名词要用单数 it。从句 it is held… 需要连接词，故选 A。D 的 during 是介系词。

**22.** I need to know __ the library is open.
- (A) that
- (B) when ✅
- (C) which
- (D) if it
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 从语法要求来看，A 和 B 都对。A 表示“图书馆开着这件事”，B 则是由疑问句变来，表示“图书馆什么时候开”，两者都是正确的名词从句。不过 B 的问题比较能配合上文 I need to know… 的语意。
