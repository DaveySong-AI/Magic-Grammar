# Test 10（第十章 介系词）题目与答案

> 提取自 https://llwslc.github.io/grammar-club/content/Chapter10.html#test
> 题目、答案与解析取自该页面渲染组件所带数据；下划线/画底线以 `<u>…</u>` 标注，选择题空格以 `__` 标注。

## 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**1.** For fear that we should run short of food __ the trip, we are carrying extra rations in the jeep.
- (A) at
- (B) among
- (C) in
- (D) on ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** the trip 是一段时间，也是一条路程，可用 on 或 along。

**2.** __ imprecise calculations, the experiment was a failure.
- (A) Due
- (B) Owing to ✅
- (C) Viewing
- (D) According
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** owing to 类似 because of，表示因果关系。A 和 D 都要加上 to 才能当短语用，C 的 viewing 不能当介系词用，只有 considering 可以这样使用。

**3.** The children came rushing __ the sound of the circus parade.
- (A) on
- (B) to
- (C) at ✅
- (D) beyond
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 用 at the sound 表示“听到声音那一刻，马上就冲出来”。

**4.** Although too much leisure may lead people to a wasteful life, everyone has a right __ a minimum amount of leisure time.
- (A) with
- (B) to ✅
- (C) on
- (D) for
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** a right to 表示“对于某事的权利”，是常用短语。

**5.** In the sentence, "The size of the room is 12' × 14'," the sign "×" is to read "__".
- (A) and
- (B) with
- (C) by ✅
- (D) cross
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 表示长宽（面积）的“×”读为 by。

**6.** The office is open Monday __ Saturday, and closed on Sundays.
- (A) since
- (B) through ✅
- (C) also
- (D) with
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** through 表示头尾包括在内，故一周中只有 Sunday 不开。

**7.** John's parents died when he was only a child, and ever since he did not seem to have a home __ his own.
- (A) in
- (B) of ✅
- (C) with
- (D) at
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 这是双重所有格，以 a home of his own 的方式来同时表示 a home 和 his own home。

**8.** The dictionary is sold __ one hundred dollars a copy.
- (A) with
- (B) by
- (C) in
- (D) at ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** “以……之单价出售”，应用 at。

**9.** The workers are paid __.
- (A) by the week ✅
- (B) with a week
- (C) to a week
- (D) since a week
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 每周计算应用 by the week。

**10.** The experts know many things that won't work in curing AIDS, so they are that much closer to __ one that will.
- (A) find
- (B) found
- (C) finding ✅
- (D) have found
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 空格前的 to 是 close to 的一部分，应当介系词看待，因而要接动名词作宾语。

**11.** __ prices so high, I'll have to do without a new suit.
- (A) With ✅
- (B) Because
- (C) Because of
- (D) As
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** with prices so high 是以介系词短语方式来减化副词从句 because prices are so high。答案 C 的后面应改为 because of high prices 方可。答案 B 和 D 都是从属连接词，可是后面的从句缺了动词。

**12.** Mrs. Johnson's old cat likes to sit __ the sun.
- (A) near
- (B) in ✅
- (C) underneath
- (D) below
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 本句中 the sun 指阳光，是立体的范围，故用 in。

**13.** You can't do a hard day's work __ a cup of coffee and a slice of bread.
- (A) of
- (B) on ✅
- (C) in
- (D) at
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** on a cup of coffee…表示“只靠一杯咖啡……（来维持体力）”。

**14.** The necklace you are wearing is very becoming __ you.
- (A) at
- (B) to ✅
- (C) for
- (D) with
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** becoming to one 表示“很适合某人（穿戴）”。

**15.** In the photograph the man's face is __ focus and blurred.
- (A) out of ✅
- (B) with
- (C) on
- (D) to
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** 因为下文说 blurred（模糊），故选 out of focus（没对好焦距）。

**16.** __ the seriousness of the occasion, the audience burst out laughing, at the extraordinary nature of the proposal.
- (A) Although
- (B) Notwithstanding ✅
- (C) In respect of
- (D) On behalf of
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 下文说观众哄堂大笑，前面则是“场合严肃”，故要表示“相反”的关系（A 或 B）。而 A 的 although 是从属连接词，不能连接名词短语 the seriousness，故用介系词的 B。

**17.** __ being portable, a walkman provides a high quality of sound.
- (A) Aside
- (B) Far from
- (C) Beside
- (D) Besides ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** “除了”可手提，还可提供高品质音响。这个“除了”是“除了这还有那” 的意思，应用 besides。C 的 beside 是“在……旁边”，A 的 aside 是副词，B 的 far from 则是“决非……”。

**18.** George likes all vegetables __ for spinach.
- (A) except ✅
- (B) accept
- (C) excuse
- (D) expect
**答案：(A)**
**解析：** except for 表示“除了……以外”，表示“这个不算”。

**19.** __ the weather, forecast or anticipated, a true English gentleman always carries an umbrella, wherever he goes.
- (A) Regardless
- (B) Regard
- (C) Regard of
- (D) Regardless of ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** regardless of 是“不顾，不管”。

**20.** I welcome you most cordially, both personally and __ behalf of the faculty and the student body.
- (A) in
- (B) at
- (C) on ✅
- (D) to
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** on behalf of 是“代表”。
