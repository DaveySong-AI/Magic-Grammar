# Test 19（第十九章 副词从句简化之一）题目与答案

## 练习一 — 将下列各句中的副词从句（即画底线部分）改写为简化从句：

**1.** 题目：1. <u>While he was watching TV</u>, the boy heard a strange noise coming from the kitchen.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>(While) watching TV</u>, the boy heard a strange noise coming from the kitchen.

**2.** 题目：2. <u>Because she lives with her parents</u>, the girl can't stay out very late.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>Living with her parents</u>, the girl can't stay out very late.

**3.** 题目：3. <u>If you have finished your work</u>, you can help me with mine.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>If having finished your work</u>，you can help me with mine.

**4.** 题目：4. <u>As he is a law-enforcement officer</u>, he cannot drink on duty.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>Being a law-enforcement officer</u>, he cannot drink on duty.

**5.** 题目：5. The actor has been in a state of excitement <u>ever since he was nominated for the Oscar</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：The actor has been in a state of excitement <u>ever since being nominated for the Oscar</u>.

**6.** 题目：6. <u>After he addressed the congregation</u>, the minister left in a hurry.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>After addressing the congregation</u>, the minister left in a hurry. 
 或 
 <u>Having addressed the congregation</u>, the minister left in a hurry.

**7.** 题目：7. <u>As it was rather warm</u>, we decided to go for a swim.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>It being rather warm</u>, we decided to go for a swim.

**8.** 题目：8. <u>When the students have all left</u>, the teacher started looking over their examination sheets.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>The students having all left</u>, the teacher started looking over their examination sheets.

**9.** 题目：9. I know all about corn farming <u>because I grew up in a Southern farm</u>.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：I know all about corn farming, <u>having grown up in a Southern farm</u>.

**10.** 题目：10. <u>As the door remained shut</u>, the servant could not hear what was going on inside.
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;答案：<u>The door remaining shut</u>, the servant could not hear what was going on inside.

## 练习二 — 请选出最适当的答案填入空格内，以使句子完整。

**11.** __ on the sofa, we began to watch television.
- (A) Sat
- (B) Seat
- (C) Seated ✅
- (D) Set
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** seat 是及物动词，本句是 we were seated on the sofa 的简化。

**12.** Returning to the room, __.
- (A) the book was lost
- (B) I found the book missing ✅
- (C) missing was book
- (D) the book was missing
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** Returning to the room 是简化从句，必须与主要从句同一主语。四个答案中只有 B 的主语是人，符合这个要求。

**13.** The average age of the Lishan apples __ today is about fifty years.
- (A) grow
- (B) grown
- (C) growing ✅
- (D) to grow
**答案：(C)**
**解析：** 空格部分是 that are growing today 的简化，成为 growing today（今天在长的）。若是 B，应为 that are grown today（今天种下去的）这一句的简化，文法也通，但是如果今天才种下去，不可能“平均年龄 50 岁”，所以不行。

**14.** Underground money lenders make most of their income from interest __ on loans.
- (A) earn
- (B) earned ✅
- (C) to earn
- (D) was earned
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 这是 that is earned on loans 这个形容词从句的简化。

**15.** __ the driveway, the house appeared to be much smaller than it had seemed to us as children many years ago.
- (A) Standing in
- (B) Seen from ✅
- (C) Crossing
- (D) Driving down
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 四个答案都是副词从句简化，条件是要与主要从句同一主语（the house），所以只有 A 或 B。the driveway 是“车道”，房子不能站在它里面，所以排除掉 A，剩下 B（When it was seen from 的简化）。

**16.** After finishing my degree, __.
- (A) my education will be employed by the university
- (B) employment will be given to me by the university
- (C) the university will employ me
- (D) I will be employed by the university ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** After finishing my degree 是 After I finish my degree 的简化，所以主要从句只能用 I 作主语，因而只有 D。

**17.** The man __ the paper is my father.
- (A) reads
- (B) reading ✅
- (C) is reading
- (D) read
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 空格以下是 who is reading the paper 的简化。

**18.** __, he washed the cup and put it away.
- (A) Drinking the coffee
- (B) Having drunk the coffee ✅
- (C) Having drank the coffee
- (D) After drank the coffee
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** A 看起来像是 when he was drinking the coffee 的简化，既然还在喝，不应洗杯子。所以选完成式的 B，表示“喝完之后”。

**19.** __ to the south of China, not far away from the coast of Mainland, Hainan Island has long played an important role in China's tourism.
- (A) Its location
- (B) Locating
- (C) Is located
- (D) Located ✅
**答案：(D)**
**解析：** 这是 Hainan Island is located to the south… 这一句的简化。

**20.** John Williams wrote his first novel __.
- (A) while he worked a porter at a hotel in Paris
- (B) while working as a porter at a hotel in Paris ✅
- (C) while worked as a porter at a hotel in Paris
- (D) while he was worked as a porter a hotel in Paris
**答案：(B)**
**解析：** 这是 while he was working as a porter… 的简化。A 中 while he worked a porter 当 worked 的宾语使用，是误把不及物的 work 当作及物动词使用。

## 答案一览（选择题）

`11.C 12.B 13.C 14.B 15.B 16.D 17.B 18.B 19.D 20.B`
